# Worknmates (OFFI)

> Worknmates, now operating as OFFI (getoffi.com), is a Latin American flexible-workspace marketplace. Companies give employees budgets to book coworking spaces, offices, and work-ready apartments near where they live; hosts list and monetize their spaces, with payouts handled through dLocal and PayPal. A 500 Global portfolio company. The product is a Spanish-language single-page app; no public developer program, API documentation, or client SDKs are published.
